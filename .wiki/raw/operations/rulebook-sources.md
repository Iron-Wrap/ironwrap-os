# Operating sources for Verification for estimate and contractor handoffs

> Source: Project operating contracts and owner-authorized rulebook remediation
> Collected: —
> Published: —

The authority contract keeps pricing, external effects and physical release within approved actions. The current capability matrix describes the evidence for estimating and identifies missing actual costing on a real job. The review exercises one handoff with a missing measurement and later compares the cost to an authorized closed record. Reusable methods can travel between organizations. Business data and approval remain with each owner.
