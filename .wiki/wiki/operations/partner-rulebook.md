# Verification for estimate and contractor handoffs

> Sources: Project operating contracts and the partner rulebook
> Raw: [Operating source record](../../raw/operations/rulebook-sources.md)
> Updated: —

## Before trusting a faster estimate

Name the estimator who can accept the draft, the job and the approval that governs its next use. Run one ordinary handoff and one case with a missing measurement using authorized data or labeled synthetic inputs. Compare comparable jobs on time, rework, unsupported quantities and missing evidence. Link every quantity to its revision. A missing value needs an explicit question for the person who can resolve it.

## Materials, approval and cost

Before a disputed material selection becomes a purchase commitment, the purchasing authority reconciles the supplier’s current product identifier, finish and approved swatch through the configuration and quote revision. Keep the disputed line open until the owner verifies it. Preserve the authorization and the provider effect for any order already created. Reconcile uncertainty before repeating a request.

When assessing estimate accuracy or margin, the estimator and job-cost accountant reconcile an authorized closed job against its issued quote revision, including materials, labour, equipment and corrections. The capability matrix records this real-job cost comparison as open. Keep a verified quote, a supplier’s order confirmation, customer acceptance and actual costs as separate records.
