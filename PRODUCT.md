# Product

## Register

product

## Users

IronWrap OS serves a low-volume Alberta roofing and metal-exteriors contractor first, with a path to white-label use by similar Canadian and U.S. operators. Owners and operations staff need to see what is stopped, what proof is missing, and what decision is next without reconstructing the job from several systems. Estimators and sales staff need traceable lead, measurement, quote, and approval context. Field crews use the PWA in sunlight, on a roof, with gloves and inconsistent connectivity, where a camera, voice input, and a large thumb target are more practical than typing.

## Product Purpose

Move each job from lead through proposal, deposit, materials, field proof, completion, invoice, and review on an owned evidence spine. The product makes operational risk and human approval explicit, preserves truthful source and sync state, and lets a contractor act quickly without inventing data or hiding a gate behind a generic dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Evidence-first, crew-proof, decisive. The product should feel calm under jobsite pressure, specific about what is known, and practical enough for a working contractor to trust with the next decision.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS metrics dashboard, a template-recognizable shadcn screen, a decorative AI control panel, or an opaque CRM activity log. Avoid fake live activity, ungrounded production claims, dashboard-card sprawl, color-only state, and roofing decoration that does not help someone act.

## Design Principles

1. Show proof before status: a claim must carry its source, freshness, or explicit uncertainty.
2. Surface the work stop first: revenue, safety, material, financial, and consent gates outrank broad reporting.
3. Make the next decision obvious: every screen answers where the user is, what is blocked, and what happens next within three seconds.
4. Respect field reality: camera and voice precede typing; offline work is visibly queued rather than treated as an error.
5. Keep human control legible: automated drafts, dry-runs, approvals, and audits remain visibly distinct from completed work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA or better with status expressed by color, icon, and words. Keep the light theme readable in direct sunlight and provide an automatic dark theme. Primary controls are at least 56 px, with 64 px targets for field use; keyboard focus, reduced motion, safe-area handling, responsive reflow, and descriptive empty and error states are required.
