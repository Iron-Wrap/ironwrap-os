# Compliance Notices — Drafts

Draft language for the Canadian compliance gates in `final-spec-pack/09` (PIPEDA, Alberta PIPA,
CASL, DNCL, call-recording). **These are drafts to accelerate legal review, NOT legal advice — a
lawyer must review and approve before production.** Fill the `[bracketed]` items per brand
(IronWrap Exteriors + iRoof Alberta have separate identities, domains, and mailing addresses).

---

## 1. Privacy notice (PIPEDA / Alberta PIPA)

> **Privacy Notice — [Brand] ([domain])**
> We collect the personal information you provide (name, phone, email, property address, service
> interest, photos, and messages) to respond to your inquiry, prepare estimates, schedule and
> complete work, process payment, and provide warranty and customer service.
>
> **Service providers / cross-border.** We use trusted service providers, some of which store or
> process data **outside Canada (primarily the United States)**: JobNimbus (CRM), Make and
> Cloudflare (lead processing/storage), Google Workspace/Drive (email/files), Microsoft 365 while
> the temporary mailbox mirror remains active, JobNimbus Engage / phone providers for calls or SMS,
> and AI document/OCR tools (e.g., Mistral). They process your information only to provide these
> services under written agreements; we remain accountable for it.
>
> **Retention.** We keep information only as long as needed for the job, accounting, warranty,
> legal, and service obligations, then delete or archive it.
>
> **Your rights.** You may request access to, or correction of, your information, or ask a question
> or make a complaint, by contacting us at **[privacy email/phone]**, **[mailing address]**.

Checklist: ☐ published on both brand sites · ☐ names the cross-border processors · ☐ contact +
complaint route · ☐ signed DPAs/ZDR on file per processor · ☐ vendor register complete.

## 2. CASL — consent, identification, unsubscribe (SMS via Engage + email via brand mailboxes)

**Web-form consent (checkbox or clear notice at submit):**
> By submitting, you agree that **[Brand]** may contact you by phone, text, and email about your
> inquiry and services. Message/data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out of texts;
> unsubscribe links are in every email.

**Identification — every commercial message must include:** the brand name (**[Brand]**), a valid
mailing address (**[address]**), and a working unsubscribe/contact method.

**SMS opt-out:** honor **STOP** immediately; **HELP** returns contact info. **Email:** one-click
unsubscribe; honor within 10 business days; stop on opt-out.

**Implied consent:** an inquiry gives time-limited implied consent for the requested service; it
does **not** authorize unrelated marketing. Record consent **basis + date + source + channel** in
the JobNimbus consent ledger (dual-written to the owned store). Stop review/nurture on any
complaint or dispute.

Checklist: ☐ consent captured at every intake · ☐ per-brand sender ID + mailing address · ☐ STOP/
HELP + unsubscribe tested · ☐ consent ledger live · ☐ A2P 10DLC approved before any automated SMS.

## 3. Call recording + AI disclosure (start-of-call)

> "Hi, you've reached **[Brand]**. You're speaking with an **automated AI assistant**, and this
> call **may be recorded and transcribed** for scheduling, quality, and our records. You can ask
> for a **person** at any time. How can I help?"

Checklist: ☐ notice plays before any capture/recording · ☐ "ask for a human" honored · ☐ recording/
transcript access limited · ☐ voicemail greeting also discloses recording.
