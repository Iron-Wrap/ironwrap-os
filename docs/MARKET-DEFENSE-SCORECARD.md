# Market Defense Scorecard

Status: verified competitive position revalidated 2026-07-10. Generated from a 19-agent research
pass over JobNimbus, GoHighLevel, Renoworks, Roofr, AccuLynx, and the measurement stack
(RoofRuler/EagleView/Hover/AppliCad/ESX), with every "exceeded" claim adversarially audited
(3 claims were downgraded to covered by that audit). Refresh via the
market-watch loop; do not edit rows by hand without re-verifying.

Honesty rule: IronWrap OS has zero live production hours, so "exceeded" means structural or
architectural superiority only (ownership, safety, economics, evidence model). Outcome and
maturity claims cannot be made until real jobs run; that is itself tracked as an open row.

Counts: 10 exceeded / 7 covered / 19 open.
