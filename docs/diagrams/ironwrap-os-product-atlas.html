Atlas reading guide

Why the product exists

Coordinate roofing, exteriors, service, shop, supply, field, finance, and compliance without turning a vendor status or AI answer into business truth.

What makes it different

Every consequential action binds actor, authority, current evidence, policy, idempotency, provider readback, and reconciliation.

How to read the maps

Red marks the focal gate or risk. Blue marks a bounded external/API path. Dashed paths are optional, blocked, or compatibility-only.

Trust architecture

People and AI may request or propose. Only the deterministic policy gate commits owned facts or permits a bounded effect. External systems remain authorities for their exact scope.

AuthorityD1 and the Worker control plane own internal operating truth.
External truthQuickBooks, Facet, issuers, and providers are authoritative only for exact scope.
AI boundaryModels propose; deterministic code evaluates, permits, executes, and reconciles.

Lead-to-proof lifecycle

One ordinary journey crosses roles, but every handoff remains a governed transition. The line continues only when the stage’s authoritative predicate is satisfied.

Commercial integrityApproval, publication, customer view, and acceptance bind one exact revision.
Physical integrityReady, assigned, started, and complete are different facts with different evidence.
Financial integrityIntent and webhook are not posting or payment; provider detail must reconcile.

Field worker: one safe day

The field experience is intentionally narrow: know today’s exact work, keep stop authority, capture evidence once, and recover safely when the network or upstream facts change.

Primary need“Tell me the exact safe work I own today, even when the network fails.”
Critical troughStops, stale gates, conflicts, and replay are where explanation and preservation matter most.
Success signalThe worker captures once; the system reconciles without duplicate work or lost evidence.

Derived job phase

The phase is a deterministic explanation of independent facts, never a command target. Blocking and cancellation win by predicate; clearing them recomputes from source facts.

ProjectionThe phase explains the most relevant current outcome; it does not own any transition.
PriorityCancellation and active safety stops override ordinary lifecycle display.
Open decisionAlign the canonical “contracted” vocabulary before exposing phase names as a stable external contract.

Do not flatten proof.

A source-visible route is useful evidence, but it is not the same claim as a configured provider, deployed runtime, accepted business journey, or real customer result.

Source-present
Executable implementation exists in the named checkout.
Configured/runtime
Named environment and dependencies pass real readback.
Business-proven
Controlled end-to-end operating scenario has accountable acceptance.
Customer-validated
Separate real-customer, legal, operational, and experience gate passes.
